Discord Username Finder

Generates 3–4 character usernames, verifies each one against Discord, and only shows the ones that are actually available.

1. What to generate

Advanced

The API URL is a small proxy you deploy once (see the repo's worker/ or api/ folder). Browsers cannot call Discord directly because of CORS. Keep the delay at 300 ms or higher to avoid being rate limited.
